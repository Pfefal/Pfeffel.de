Only a small named selection of main roads is shown. Hydro areas smaller than 10,000 square metres omitted. Offshore sea extent is the source's FuzzyExtentWaterArea, not a surveyed boundary. No endorsement by NSW Spatial Services is implied. Informational context only, not for navigation.

Projection: longitude/latitude mapped linearly into SVG viewBox 0 0 1000 620; north is up. Display is not a distance-preserving projection.

Site redesign adds original approximate locality-name labels (not venue coordinates) and adjusts line contrast. Labels are orientation aids, not navigation instructions.


Compact map revision (25 October 2026): existing water/coast geometry and labels retained unchanged. City water geometry had previously been generalised at 0.65 SVG pixels; wide geometry at 2.5 pixels, omitting water rings below 20 square SVG pixels.

Expanded light road layer (25 October 2026): selected main roads from the same NSW Transport Theme RoadSegment source are clipped to the existing map bounds, joined at coincident endpoints, simplified at 1.1 SVG pixels (city) or 2 pixels (wide), and rounded to whole SVG pixels. Very short segments omitted. No new data service or runtime dependency.
