Week: 26 October–1 November 2026. Event snapshot: 25 October, Sydney time.

EVENTS AND ARTISTS
The source research contains 34 individual shows from 31 taste-ranked groups. This website presents only the first 15 individual shows in that existing fit order. The calendar and playlist are not capped by this website setting. Every expanded card includes its verified official event/ticket link and source note. Different dates and support bills remain separate. Availability is a snapshot, not a guarantee or reservation; prices marked unconfirmed are not invented. Compact prices in closed rows are summaries; full tier/fee details remain in the expanded card. Optional calendar files preserve known source times, which may be doors; unknown end times are not invented.

Spotify artist links were matched to the exact previously verified playlist tracks, not guessed from names. Ambiguous artists remain unlinked. Tribute shows do not link to the original artist as a substitute. The playlist is the user's existing public playlist. Spotify is a trusted, explicitly permitted external exception: an artist player loads automatically when its gig is expanded, as explicitly requested, with no automatic playback. GENRES
The coloured regions are an original editorial guide to related styles, not a formal taxonomy or measured musical axes. Each gig appears once by dominant sound; many bills cross genres. No Map of Metal code, images, design or database is copied.

DESIGN AND DEPENDENCIES
Original HTML/CSS/JavaScript, system fonts, local map files and the user-authorized original generated playlist cover. No frontend library, build runtime, CDN font, analytics or Spotify SDK. Professional reference research examined Bandsintown's artist/date/venue hierarchy and RA's event hierarchy, not their protected assets. DICE and Songkick references were unavailable in the research tool; RA's live browser check stopped at its device verification. None of their code/images was copied. This edition uses a compact playlist feature, a neutral interface with a warm accent, and only Featured week/Today/This weekend date controls. Selected filters use the actual Australia/Sydney date, not the featured-week date.

The MIT licence applies only to original application code and layout. Geographic data, point-data licences, Spotify content/marks and other linked content retain their own rights. Offline use is no longer a user requirement. The page still avoids third-party requests at initial load; external links and the optional player require internet.
