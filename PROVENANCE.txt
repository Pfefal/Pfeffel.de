SYDNEY GIGS — SOURCES AND ATTRIBUTION
Week: 26 October–1 November 2026. Event snapshot: 25 October, Sydney time.

EVENTS AND ARTISTS
34 individual shows from 31 taste-ranked research groups. Every expanded card includes its verified official event/ticket link and source note. Different dates and support bills remain separate. Availability is a snapshot, not a guarantee or reservation; prices marked unconfirmed are not invented. Compact prices in closed rows are summaries; full tier/fee details remain in the expanded card. Optional calendar files preserve known source times, which may be doors; unknown end times are not invented.

Spotify artist links were matched to the exact previously verified playlist tracks, not guessed from names. Ambiguous artists remain unlinked. Tribute shows do not link to the original artist as a substitute. The playlist is the user's existing public playlist. Spotify is a trusted, explicitly permitted external exception: an artist player is loaded only on request, with no automatic playback. Spotify-hosted content and trademarks are not relicensed by this project. See PRIVACY.txt and the Spotify widget terms.

Venue coordinates are approximate venue/address points, not entrance/navigation guarantees. Their separate provenance and attribution are included with geography.js and any accompanying venue-data licence. No unverified venue is substituted with a suburb centroid. Each numbered marker is an individual show, matching its number in the list. Overlapping markers are separated for readability and connected back to venue locations by leader lines. The smaller viewport can be panned horizontally. Inner Sydney and All Sydney views use distinct real geographic bounds.

GENRES
The coloured regions are an original editorial guide to related styles, not a formal taxonomy or measured musical axes. Each gig appears once by dominant sound; many bills cross genres. No Map of Metal code, images, design or database is copied.

DESIGN AND DEPENDENCIES
Original HTML/CSS/JavaScript, system fonts and local map files. No frontend library, build runtime, CDN font, analytics or Spotify SDK. Professional reference research examined Bandsintown's artist/date/venue hierarchy and RA's event hierarchy, not their protected assets. DICE and Songkick references were unavailable in the research tool; RA's live browser check stopped at its device verification. None of their code/images was copied. This redesign intentionally omits a large poster/hero, search or filter toolbar.

The MIT licence applies only to original application code and layout. Geographic data, point-data licences, Spotify content/marks and other linked content retain their own rights. Offline use is no longer a user requirement. The page still avoids third-party requests at initial load; external links and the optional player require internet.
