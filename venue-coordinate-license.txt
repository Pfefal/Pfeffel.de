Changes: selected venue/address matches for this gig guide; transformed latitude/longitude into the displayed map projection. All positions are approximate venue/property points, not verified entrances, accessibility routes or navigation directions. Distinct events may share the same source point; graphical marker separation does not alter the underlying venue coordinates.

Data was collected once through a rate-limited, single-threaded research lookup and cached locally. There is no Nominatim, OSM, map tile or other geocoding API request in the running website. The research helper is not part of the shipped runtime and no periodic geocoding is configured. Nominatim usage restrictions: https://operations.osmfoundation.org/policies/nominatim/ . Future research should reuse cached points and comply with the provider's then-current policy; do not turn this into a public geocoding/search endpoint.
